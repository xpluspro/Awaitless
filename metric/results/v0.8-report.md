## Fault Findings

80/100, and shell 60/100. Recovery was Awaitless, strong wrapper, and native tmux
20/20; plain shell recovered 0/20. The large-log cells account for the baseline
fidelity failures. Usage tokens are unavailable for this non-LLM matrix and are not
inferred from bytes.

## Agent And Spectrum Findings

All three real-Agent arms completed the targeted pytest workload in all 20 paired
trials. Awaitless used a median of two Agent-visible calls, equal to the strong
wrapper and one fewer than native tmux. The strong wrapper used fewer model tokens in
this workload; v0.8 therefore does not claim a universal token advantage.

The spectrum suite completed all 25 shell and 25 Awaitless trials. `git status` and
`rg` stayed inline; the full suite crossed the adaptive boundary and detached
automatically. Both arms returned the correct result contract in every trial.
